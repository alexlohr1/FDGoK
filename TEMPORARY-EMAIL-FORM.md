# Temporary email-draft appointment form

Both English and Spanish contact pages now open a prefilled email addressed to info@floridadentalgroup.com. The visitor must press **Send** in their own email application. No email is sent by the website; no submissions are saved on the server.

## Limitations
- Requires an email app configured on the visitor’s device. Some browser/device combinations may do nothing. The page provides the clinic phone as fallback.
- This approach is not appropriate for protected health information. Do not request symptoms, diagnoses, or other clinical details.
- This cannot reliably measure successfully sent inquiries or provide a submission confirmation.
- Replace with a vetted, privacy-appropriate form service or Open Dental workflow after checking vendor agreements and handling of patient information. A generic Formspree account is not automatically HIPAA compliant.
